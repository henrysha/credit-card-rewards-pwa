# Hilton and IHG card sources

Verified September 3, 2026 against primary issuer and loyalty-program pages. Offers are time-sensitive and may change after this update.

- [Hilton Honors American Express cards (Hilton)](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/credit-cards/) — US personal Hilton (80,000 points), Surpass (130,000 points), and Aspire (150,000 points) welcome offers, annual fees, earning rates, status, and credits.
- [Hilton Honors American Express Card (Amex)](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/credit-cards/card/hilton-honors/) — no-fee card earning rates, Silver status, and no current Free Night Reward.
- [Hilton Honors American Express Aspire Card (Amex)](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/credit-cards/card/hilton-honors-aspire/) — $200 annual flight credit applies to qualifying flight purchases made directly with an airline or through Amex Travel, not incidental airline fees.
- [IHG One Rewards cards (Chase)](https://creditcards.chase.com/travel-credit-cards/ihg) — US consumer Premier and Traveler annual fees, current welcome offers, earning rates, status, free-night, fourth-night, and travel benefits.

Family eligibility is informational in this release: the Churning page reports prior history, but adding a card still creates its normal sign-up-bonus tracker. Product changes are the exception and are enforced as same-issuer, same-family mutations both through the UI and in the database helper.

Business products are intentionally excluded from the catalog because this release covers US consumer cards.
