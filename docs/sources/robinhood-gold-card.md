# Robinhood Gold Card source notes

Checked September 3, 2026. The app reflects the controlling legal terms in
effect on that date:

- [Robinhood Gold Card Rewards Program Rules](https://api.robinhood.com/creditcard/legal/reward-terms), last updated September 1, 2026: eligible purchases earn 3 points per dollar, including purchases made through the Robinhood Travel Portal, which are “no longer eligible for extra Points.”
- [Robinhood Gold Card Rewards Program Rules PDF](https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/Robinhood_Gold_Card_Rewards_Program_Rules.pdf), last updated July 29, 2026: the same 3x Travel Portal treatment appears in the hosted legal document.
- [Apply for the Robinhood Gold Card](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/apply-for-robinhood-credit-card/): the card has no annual fee, requires an annual Robinhood Gold subscription ($50) to apply and maintain it, and does not include a 30-day free trial.
- [Robinhood Gold Card rewards](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/robinhood-gold-card-rewards/): still advertises up to 5x on select Travel Portal purchases, but provides no effective date and conflicts with the newer controlling Rewards Program Rules. It is not used as the source for the current multiplier.

Therefore the seed data represents one 3x catch-all rate that includes the
Travel Portal and does not claim a 5x portal bonus.
